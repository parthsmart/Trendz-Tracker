💡 How Oral Rounds work
  • Listen: you hear the question type (for example "2 digit times 2 digit"), then the first number. After your chosen delay you hear the second number.
  • Answer: type the answer and press Enter. You see straight away whether it is right.
  • Practice only: your score stays on this screen. It is not saved and does not count toward the leaderboard or your streak.
