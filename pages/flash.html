💡 How Flash Anzan Trainer works

Flash Anzan tests your ability to visualize and calculate rapid flashing numbers mentally. Numbers flash on the screen one at a time at your chosen speed. When the sequence finishes, type your final total.

  • Settings: Choose number of digits, sequence length, and speed in seconds per number.
  • Scoring: Completed tries are automatically timed and recorded by the site to build your streak.
  • Pace: Start at a comfortable speed (e.g. 1.0s or 1.5s) and gradually increase speed as your visualization sharpens.
