Simple

Simple sums you can do on the abacus without any formula, and tables in random order. Practise online or log a score from paper.

💡 How Simple works
  • Simple sums (1D 4R, 2D 2R, 3D 2R): every row can be added or taken away on the abacus directly, with no small or big friends. Plus and minus rows are mixed.
  • Tables dodging: one table (2 to 9) with the multipliers 1 to 10 in random order, for example 2 × 3, then 2 × 7.
  • Advanced: 2–5 mix and 2–9 mix ask from several tables at random.
  • Online: pick how long the round runs (30 seconds to 5 minutes, or unlimited) and answer as many questions as you can. Answers are marked at the end, not one by one. When time runs out, the question on screen does not count. Your score is saved when the round ends.
  • Offline: did a sheet on paper? Log the score from the last 7 days.
